STEP 01
🌊Read the Wave
Zoom out chart to smallest view
Analyze last 600 bars from current candle
Wave first — candle analysis second
Identify wave shape
Case 1: Strong breakout · Case 2: V-shape · Case 3: I-shape long trend
Locate the latest / most recent trend
Look for strong breakout that invalidated prior structure
V-shape: analyze both down + up waves left-to-right
Confirm down trend is finished before looking for up entries
STEP 02
📈Define the Trend
Mark the Key Level (KL) on chart KL
Only KL defines trend — not POI zone
Price below KL → trend is DOWN (sell bias)
Price above KL → trend is UP (buy bias)
Price just broke KL → trend has changed
Adjust bias immediately on confirmed KL break
STEP 03
⚡Assess Momentum (MMT)
Identify if trend momentum is strong or weak
Strong MMT = CKL or SMC · Weak MMT = UKL strategy
Strong KL = momentum favors continuation
Breaking a strong KL = trend changes
MOMENTUM IS THE CORE OF NCI SYSTEM
STEP 04
✅Qualify KL Zone (FMC)
F1 FRESH — Zone never visited before KL
Fibo: if price bounced from discount 61.8-80% → UN-FRESH, skip
Fresh check: market bounced, not just tapped
F2 MOMENTUM — Angle of wave ≥ 67.5°
Measure: lowest pullback low – drag to recent high. Angle > IMB always.
IMB size ≥ biggest Maru in last 5 Marus on left
Compound adjacent IMBs unless reversal Maru separates them
F3 CLEAN BASE — No equal highs/lows in pullback
Equal C=A and D=B → Unclean BASE, skip entry
STEP 05
🎯Qualify POI Zone (FMC)
POI is Fresh — market has not tested it yet POI
POI is inside Discount zone (61.8–80% Fibo)
POI = strongest tradeable zone in discount. Not for trend direction.
Institutional Candle (IC) present in discount
Best: Up Maru → Down Maru IC → Up Maru · Also valid: Maru → Pinbar → Maru
IC size ≥ 50% of next Maru to right (30% if next Maru very large)
Candle before + after IC are both Maru candles
IMB right after IC = strongest POI signal of all
STEP 06
💥Shock Move — UKL Only
Confirmation needed ONLY for UKL (counter-trend) UKL
Shock Move (SM): strong wave with IMB inside, 3–5+ candles SM
Best: 4 big Marubozu moving continuously one direction
SM angle of wave ≥ 67.5°
Measure from highest high of SM to take-profit level
SM is NOT formed after a range
Post-range Maru = buyers exiting, not a valid shock move
Special: single very large Maru qualifies if much bigger than prior candles
STEP 07
💰Take Profit with MMT — NCI 2026 Standard
Identify potential TP levels (next KL, next POI)
Measure angle: pulse wave start → TP level A
Higher angle = faster & safer arrival
Measure angle: pulse wave start → TP level B (further target)
Choose TP with angle ≥ 67.5° — higher angle always preferred
Do NOT chase the full cycle — take the best, safest section only
